About the character video tool

Pick a character you built and saved in the Character Studio, choose a voice (record and convert your own, clone one, or use the default), and make a realistic clip — either the character speaking to camera with lips matched to the audio, or a short action shot. Videos take a minute or two; the tool keeps working and shows the result when it's ready.

Free to use. Media is sent to the AI services only to produce your video and isn't stored by Toolbench. Note that AI video can drift on identity or hands in longer or action clips — talking-to-camera is the most reliable.
