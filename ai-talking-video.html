About the talking character video tool

Record yourself performing the line, and the tool converts your recording into your chosen voice while keeping your exact timing, pauses and emotion (this is voice-to-voice conversion, not flat text reading). It then animates the character so the lips match the audio. Prefer typing? Switch to “Type text” and the voice reads your script instead. Arabic and many other languages are supported.

It’s free to use. Audio and images are sent to the AI services only to produce your video and are not stored by Toolbench.
