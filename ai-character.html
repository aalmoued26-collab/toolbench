About the AI character studio

Upload a single photo and build a reusable character: a full set of shots from every side, in the outfit and scene you describe, with the same face kept consistent. Save the character on your device, then reuse it — including in the talking video tool to make it speak and move. Change the outfit or scene and generate again to create new looks.

Free to use. Generation happens on a secure AI service; your uploaded photo is used only to build your character and isn’t kept by Toolbench.
