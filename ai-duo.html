About the two-character scene tool

Choose two characters you built in the Character Studio and the tool composes them into one photorealistic scene together, keeping each face recognizable. You can then animate the scene into a short clip. Great for a grandmother and granddaughter cooking, a founder and a customer, or any pairing.

Free to use. Note: putting two AI faces in one shot is harder than one — pick clear front-facing shots for the best likeness, and expect the still image to hold identity better than the animated clip.
