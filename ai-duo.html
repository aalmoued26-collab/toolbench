About the two-character scene tool

Choose two characters you built in the Character Studio, pick the exact saved photo to use for each, give them outfits, type any background/location and a framing style, and the tool composes them into one photorealistic scene together — keeping each face recognizable. You can then animate the scene into a short clip. Great for a grandmother and granddaughter cooking, a founder and a customer, or a two-person selfie vlog.

For a vlog, choose the “Selfie / vlog POV” framing so one character holds the phone and both faces are in frame, then hit Animate for the handheld motion. A true camera-flip where two people trade spoken lines in one continuous shot isn’t something today’s AI can render as a single clip — the reliable way is to animate this scene for the b-roll, then use the Talking Video tool to make each person speak their line lip-synced, and cut those together in any editor.

Free to use. Note: putting two AI faces in one shot is harder than one — pick clear front-facing shots for the best likeness, and expect the still image to hold identity better than the animated clip.
